# Third-Party Notices

Jerkgram is based on Telegram for iOS and includes upstream and third-party components that may carry their own copyright notices and license terms.

## Policy

For each Stable public source snapshot:

1. preserve applicable notices already present in the corresponding source tree;
2. preserve license files shipped with included dependencies/submodules;
3. do not replace component-specific licenses with the Jerkgram project license;
4. review any Jerkgram-added third-party component before publication.

This top-level file is an index/policy notice. The authoritative component-specific notices remain the license and notice files contained alongside the corresponding source/dependency tree.
