# Support Jerkgram

## Community

For beta feedback, installation discussion, bug reports and general project conversation:

**[@JerkgramCommunity](https://t.me/JerkgramCommunity)**

Stable announcements are published through **[@JerkgramApp](https://t.me/JerkgramApp)**.

## Hardware sponsorship

Hardware sponsorship is welcome when it directly improves Jerkgram development, build iteration, debugging or on-device testing.

The project does not require sponsorship in order to keep its public source available. Any future sponsorship information should remain clearly separate from release/source integrity.
